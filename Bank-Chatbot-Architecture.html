Employee AI assistant · research note

How a bank chatbot for employees works

How a typical employee chatbot for a bank is built. Staff ask questions in plain language. The assistant answers from the bank's own circulars and SOPs, cites the source, and hands over to a human expert when it is unsure.

1 · Architecture

Where every part lives

In a typical setup, everything runs inside the bank's network or an India-region private cloud. The orchestrator is the centre: it is the only component that talks to the AI models and the document library. Employees never reach those directly.

Orchestrator CODE

Software the bank builds or buys. Runs every step in order, decides where each question goes, calls all other parts.

SSO gateway CODE

Confirms the employee. Their role decides which documents they may see.

Safety + PII models AI

Hide any customer account number, PAN or Aadhaar typed in. Catch "ignore your rules" style misuse.

Intent classifier AI

Labels the question (e.g. KYC policy, loan documents, account opening) with a confidence score.

Document search (RAG) AI

Circulars and SOPs split into passages and indexed. An embedding model finds passages by meaning.

LLM gateway + LLM AI

The LLM writes the answer from the passages it is given. The gateway lets the bank swap models.

Memory + audit log CODE

Short-term chat context, and a permanent record of every question, source and answer.
