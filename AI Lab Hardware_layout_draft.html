Power, room and security

  • Power: about 1.0 kW at peak (up to 385 W per Mac Studio, per Apple), 0.5–0.6 kW normally.
  • Circuits: two 16A circuits with earthing, one PDU each. Studio 1 and Mac mini on A, Studio 2 and switch on B.
  • Cords: original Apple power cords, PDU outlets to match. No UPS; the Macs restart automatically after a power cut.
  • Room: 22–24°C (never above 27°C), 40–60% humidity, central AC on 24×7. Keep 10 cm clear behind each Mac.
  • Security: about 10U in a lockable cabinet, in an access-controlled room. FileVault on; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off.
